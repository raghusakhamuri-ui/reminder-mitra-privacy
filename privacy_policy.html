Privacy Policy

Reminder Mitra

Reminder Mitra (“the app”) keeps your reminders on your phone. We have no account system and no server that receives your reminders, notes or voice.

What stays on your phone

Your reminders, notes, settings and voice recordings made with the Voice button stay on your phone. Nothing is synced to an account. If Google backup is on, Android may back this data up and restore it after you reinstall the app. We do not control that backup.

Microphone and voice

The microphone is used only for voice features you choose: Tap to Speak, the notification microphone, notes, Free Hand, and stopping a ringing alarm by voice (Premium Plus). Free Hand listens for the wake word “Mitra” only while it is on, and shows a notification while it runs. The wake word is detected on your phone. To turn speech into text, the app uses your phone's speech recognition: on your phone when available, otherwise Google's speech recognition service. The app does not send your audio anywhere else. You can switch Free Hand off at any time in the app or in its notification.

Spoken text on screen

If you switch on “Show spoken text over other apps” (Premium and Premium Plus), the text you just said is shown for a few seconds over other apps. This is optional and needs the “Display over other apps” permission. The text is not stored or sent.

Ads (Free version only)

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. AdMob may collect your advertising ID, IP address, approximate location, ad interactions and app performance data to show and measure ads. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone settings. Premium and Premium Plus show no ads. Free users in some regions are first shown a Google consent form for ads. You can change that choice at any time in Settings > Privacy choices.

Subscriptions

Premium and Premium Plus are paid through Google Play. We do not receive or store your payment card details.

Other services

The app has no analytics or crash-reporting tools of its own. The voice model for Free Hand is downloaded once from Google Play.

Permissions

Microphone: voice features. Notifications and exact alarms: to show and ring your reminders on time. Display over other apps: the optional spoken-text view. Internet: ads, subscriptions and the voice model download. Other permissions (start after restart, keep running, vibrate, full-screen alarm, battery optimisation) only keep alarms and Free Hand working.

Your choices

You can delete any reminder or note in the app. You can switch off Free Hand, the notification microphone and the spoken-text view in the app. You can withdraw any permission in your phone settings. You can cancel a subscription in Google Play.

Children

The app is not intended for children.

Changes

We may update this policy. The new text will appear in the app.

Contact

Contact us through the support channel listed on the app's Google Play Store page.
