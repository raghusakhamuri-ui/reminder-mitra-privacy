Privacy Policy

Reminder Mitra

In short: Reminder Mitra does not collect, transmit, or store your personal data on any server of ours. All reminders, notes, and settings are kept locally on your device only. Speech recognition and ads are handled by Google services, as described below. We do not use any analytics or crash-reporting service.

Reminder Mitra ("we", "our", "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app handles, how it is used, and the choices you have.

Information We Collect

Reminder Mitra stores the reminders you create — including titles, dates, times, and categories — entirely on your device using a local database. We do not upload your reminder content to any external server, and we have no backend server that receives this data.

To function correctly, the app requests permissions for exact alarms, notifications, vibration, and (optionally) microphone access for voice-based reminder creation. How voice features work is described in the "Voice and microphone" section below.

How We Use Information

Your reminder data is used solely to schedule and display notifications and alarms on your device.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, share, or transmit your personal reminder data to any third party. The app does not include any analytics or crash-reporting service. The only third-party services involved are your phone's speech recognition service, Google AdMob (ads) and Google Play (subscriptions), as described in the sections below.

Voice and microphone

Reminder Mitra uses the microphone only for voice features you choose to use: Tap to Speak, the notification microphone, notes, Free Hand, and (Premium Plus) stopping a ringing alarm by voice. Free Hand listens continuously for the wake word "Mitra" while it is switched on and shows a notification while it is running; the wake word is detected on your device. After that, and for the other voice features, your speech is converted to text by your phone's speech recognition service: on your device when supported, otherwise by Google's speech recognition service. Reminder Mitra does not record or store your audio. You can switch Free Hand off at any time from the app or the notification.

Spoken text on screen

If you turn on "Show spoken text over other apps" (Premium and Premium Plus), the text of what you just said is shown for a few seconds over the app you are using, so you can check it. This uses Android's "Display over other apps" permission, is optional and is off until you allow it. The text is not stored or sent anywhere.

Ads and subscriptions

The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, ad interactions and app performance information to show and measure ads and prevent fraud. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings. Premium and Premium Plus remove ads. Premium and Premium Plus are paid subscriptions processed by Google Play; we do not receive or store your payment card details. We do not use any other analytics or crash-reporting service.

Local-Only Storage

All app data — reminders, notes, settings, and preferences — lives only in a local database on your device. Nothing is synced to the cloud or to any account. If you back up your device through your phone manufacturer's or Android's own backup mechanism, that backup is controlled by you and by that backup provider, not by us.

Your Choices

You can delete any reminder at any time from within the app. Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored data. You may also revoke permissions such as microphone or notification access at any time from your device Settings.

Children's Privacy

Reminder Mitra is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect any information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the app. Continued use of the app after changes are published constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us through the support channel listed on the app's Google Play Store page.
